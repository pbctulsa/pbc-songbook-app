## Description

PBC Songbook makes the Peniel Baptist Church song collection easy to use wherever people gather for worship.

Find songs by title, number, book, or words from the lyrics. Open a song in a clear, adjustable reader, save favorites, copy or share lyrics, and keep the screen awake while singing. The complete songbook is included for offline use, and published corrections can be downloaded when a connection is available.

If you notice a mistake, submit a suggested edit for church review directly from the song. PBC Songbook has no advertising, analytics, or account requirement.

PBC Songbook is a ministry of Peniel Baptist Church in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

## App Review notes

PBC Songbook is a native song reader for Peniel Baptist Church's published collection. It includes search by title, number, book, and lyrics; offline access; favorites; adjustable reading appearance; copy and share actions; and a moderated correction-suggestion workflow.

No login is required. The app is free and contains no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or paid digital content. The optional donation button opens Peniel Baptist Church's external Church Center page in the system browser; donations do not unlock app content or functionality.

Suggested edits are sent only after the user taps Submit. Name and email are optional. Submitted edits enter the church's review queue and never alter published songs directly.
